LUCKNOW SUPER GIANTS

Logo : LSG

The Lucknow Super Giants (LSG) logo is a vibrant and dynamic representation of the team's spirit, featuring a pair of wings that symbolize speed, ambition, and growth. Inspired by the colors of the Indian national flag, the logo incorporates saffron, white, and green, reflecting patriotism and energy. At its center, a red cricket ball and a blue bat-like structure emphasize the team's strong cricketing identity. The bold typography of the team’s name adds a modern and powerful touch, while the design subtly incorporates elements of the RP-Sanjiv Goenka Group’s branding. Overall, the LSG logo embodies the franchise’s competitive spirit, passion, and commitment to excellence in the IPL.

Owner : Sanjiv Goenka

Sanjiv Goenka born on January 29, 1961, in Kolkata, is a prominent Indian industrialist and the chairman of the RP-Sanjiv Goenka Group, a diversified conglomerate with interests spanning power, retail, IT, and media. He earned his Bachelor of Commerce degree from St. Xavier's College, Kolkata, in 1981. EN.WIKIPEDIA.ORG In the sports arena, Goenka has made significant investments. He owns the Indian Premier League (IPL) franchise Lucknow Super Giants (LSG), which he acquired in 2021 for ₹7,090 crore, marking the most expensive franchise purchase in IPL history at that time. EN.WIKIPEDIA.ORG Beyond cricket, his group has ventured into football by acquiring a majority stake in the historic Mohun Bagan Football Club, rebranding it as Mohun Bagan Super Giants.

Captain : Rishabh Pant

Rishabh Pant, born on October 4, 1997, in Roorkee, Uttarakhand, is an Indian cricketer renowned for his dynamic batting and wicket-keeping skills. He began his cricket journey at the Sonnet Cricket Academy in Delhi under the mentorship of coach Tarak Sinha. Pant made his international debut in a T20I against England on February 1, 2017. He is celebrated for his aggressive batting style, notably scoring a century in England during his third Test match, making him the first Indian wicket-keeper to achieve this feat. In the Indian Premier League (IPL), he has been a pivotal player for the Delhi Capitals and, as of 2025, captains the Lucknow Super Giants. Beyond his on-field achievements, Pant's resilience and dedication have made him a prominent figure in Indian cricket.
